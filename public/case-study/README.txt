Put your own sketches, wireframe photos or screenshots here, then list them under `sketchImages`
in src/data/caseStudy.ts, for example:

  sketchImages: [
    { src: '/case-study/onboarding-sketch.jpg', caption: 'First sketch of the onboarding flow' },
  ],

They appear automatically in section 05 (Exploration). Use descriptive captions: they are also the image alt text.
